## 2. Digital Presence Patterns

### Hero / welcome messaging
UK local parent sites consistently use **warm, understated, first-person-plural** voice. Typical pattern: short tagline + one sentence on who's welcome + one sentence on what you get.

- **Gathered (SE London):** *"Conversation, Connection & Community for South East London Mums"* — emphasises "come just as you are" and "all experiences are welcome here". Uses relatable phrases like "eye-burning sleep deprivation".
- **NCT Dulwich:** *"NCT Dulwich and district welcomes every new parent. Our local groups, activities and events are run by volunteers."*
- **SNC:** *"We are a creative and innovative London-wide social enterprise created by a team of experienced parent carers to provide support, empower and connect families."*
- **Happity:** mission-led — *"end parent loneliness once and for all"* — founders' story (two mums) prominent.

Common traits: founder/origin story near the top; validation over aspiration; explicit welcome of all experiences; plain English.

### Activity / meetup listings
- Card grids with image, title, day, location (NCT, Happity, Gathered)
- Weekly repeating format ("Monday", "Thursday" pages — East Dulwich Mummy)
- External booking platforms commonly used (Gathered books through Happity; many forums use Eventbrite)
- Postcode search is standard on national platforms

### WhatsApp / community group joining
Three common patterns observed:
1. **Gated by email/form** — most common for borough forums (membership form first, WhatsApp invite follows). Protects safeguarding.
2. **Contact a number/person to request invite** — City Parent Carer Forum pattern
3. **Optional add-on after booking** — Gathered pattern ("optional, but often very helpful")

Direct WhatsApp join-links on public pages are rare (safeguarding concern for groups with children).

### Events display
- Upcoming events list with date/time/venue
- Filters by age band (very common — baby / toddler / primary / SEND)
- "Regular weekly" vs "one-off" distinction

### Newsletter / membership signup
- Simple email field in footer, one-line privacy reassurance (e.g. Gathered: *"We respect your privacy and will never share your details with anyone."*)
- Mailchimp is dominant
- Membership often free; signup is the primary conversion action on homepage

### Testimonials
- Short first-name quotes (2–4 sentences)
- Emotional-validation-led — the standout pattern is *"someone else gets it, it's not just me"* (Gathered)
- Usually 3–4 quotes in a row, often with photo or just initials

### Photography vs illustrations
- **Candid lifestyle photography** dominates (groups of parents, hands, coffee cups, prams) — not stock
- Illustrations used by national platforms (Happity, Hoop) and for icons/features
- Local groups favour real member photos; larger platforms use commissioned illustration for scalability

## 3. UK-Specific Conventions & Expectations

### GDPR / privacy
- Dedicated **Privacy Policy** page is standard (UK GDPR requirement)
- Cookie banner expected
- Plain-English privacy notice — ICO guidance: "concise, clear and plain style, age-appropriate"
- Explicit statement that data isn't shared/sold
- Lawful basis often cited (legitimate interest for community comms, consent for marketing)

### Safeguarding statements
**Very important** in UK parent groups. Expectations:
- Named safeguarding / child protection lead
- Short public-facing safeguarding statement (linked from footer)
- Policy reference to DBS checks for volunteers in direct contact with children
- Example: City Family CIC publishes a Safeguarding Policy Statement covering child protection leads, best-practice procedures, and secure record-keeping in line with data protection legislation.

### Accessibility statements
- Increasingly standard (WCAG 2.1 AA referenced)
- Usually linked in footer alongside Privacy/Cookies/Safeguarding

### "Parents and carers" language
- Strongly increasing as UK standard. Used by: Waltham Forest PF, Lambeth PF, Lewisham PCF, Gateshead PCF, City PCF, SNC, Greenwich PCPF.
- Inclusive of foster carers, kinship carers, grandparents raising children, SEND parent-carers
- GPC's name already reflects this — a real strength versus many "Mums"/"Mummies" groups.

### CIC badging & Companies House
- Standard practice: display **"[Name] CIC"** in footer
- Show **Companies House number** in footer (GPC: 16387545; SNC: 11123977; Greenwich PCPF became CIC March 2022)
- Registered office address in footer
- Sometimes a short "What is a CIC?" explainer on About page

### School-year cohorts / NHS cohort groupings
- Groupings by **Reception year / school year** are a UK-specific pattern (e.g. "Reception 2027 starters")
- **NHS cohort groups**: "Babies born Spring 2025" or by birth month — aligns with NHS health-visitor cohorting
- WhatsApp groups commonly split by these cohorts

### Mentions of local infrastructure
Local groups routinely signpost to:
- Local council (borough family services / Family Information Service / Local Offer)
- **Children's Centres / Family Hubs** (Camberwell & Dulwich Family Hub pattern)
- **Health visitors** and NHS clinics
- **SENDIASS** (for SEND forums)
- Local GP / midwifery services
Signposting is treated as a core service, not an extra.

## 4. Features GPC May Be Missing

Based on comparator analysis:

1. **Local business / family-friendly directory** — East Dulwich Mummy and NCT local branches both curate this; Netmums built its brand on it. High-value, low-effort.
2. **SEND resources hub** — links to RB Greenwich SEND Local Offer, SENDIASS, Greenwich PCPF partnership.
3. **Baby-loss support signposting** — Sands, Tommy's, SOS Baby Loss (oursam.org.uk) — a standard, compassionate inclusion.
4. **Parent/carer mental-health support** — PANDAS Foundation (PND), Mind Greenwich, Tommy's, NHS Talking Therapies Greenwich.
5. **School admission info** — Reception/secondary admission deadlines, school catchments, open day calendar (highly searched locally).
6. **Holiday childcare lists** — Easter/summer/half-term holiday clubs in Greenwich (a known parent pain point).
7. **NHS cohort / school-year WhatsApp groupings** — explicit sign-up landing for "Babies born [month/year]" or "Reception 2027" cohorts.
8. **Children's Centre / Family Hub signposting** — RB Greenwich hubs with addresses/hours.
9. **Safeguarding statement** in footer (if not already present).
10. **Accessibility statement** in footer.
11. **"Meet the volunteers"** page — builds trust, standard for CICs.
12. **Partner logos** (council, NHS, local charities) — credibility signal.
13. **Annual Community Report / Impact page** — CIC best practice; required in CIC34 report to Regulator.
14. **Bilingual signposting / translation note** (Greenwich has significant Nepali, Vietnamese, Somali, Polish communities).

## 5. Tone-of-Voice Extracts (verbatim)

### Gathered (SE London mums community)
> "Conversation, Connection & Community for South East London Mums" … "come just as you are" … "all experiences are welcome here."

Validating, non-prescriptive, acknowledges the hard bits ("eye-burning sleep deprivation", "low-brow Netflix favourites") — avoids aspirational parenting language.

### NCT Dulwich & District (volunteer local branch)
> "NCT Dulwich and district welcomes every new parent. Our local groups, activities and events are run by volunteers."

Short, inclusive, volunteer-forward. "Every new parent" signals welcome without over-qualifying.

### Special Needs Community (London CIC)
> "We are a creative and innovative London-wide social enterprise created by a team of experienced parent carers to provide support, empower and connect families."

"By parent-carers, for parent-carers" framing — strong credibility signal. Verbs "support, empower, connect" are a common UK community trio.

### Privacy reassurance example (Gathered footer)
> "We respect your privacy and will never share your details with anyone."

One-line footer privacy reassurance — a small but standard trust signal.
