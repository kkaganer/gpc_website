# 8.1: Privacy page rewritten to the required disclosure IA

**Story ID:** 8.1
**Epic:** 8 — Data rights and privacy
**Slug:** privacy-page-update
**Status:** ready-for-dev

> Owned by planning until `ready-for-dev`. Sized to one dev-day (~2-8h). No story points.

## Story

As **the data controller (Aster Thackery)**,
I want **`/privacy` restructured to the required information-architecture with every section a
member can act on wired up**,
so that **the site's disclosures can be made to match what the form actually collects and
publishes before `/join` accepts its first submission**.

## Acceptance Criteria

<!-- LOCKED. External dev tools must not edit Acceptance Criteria. -->

1. Given `/privacy` is requested, then the page renders exactly one `<h1>` reading "How we handle
   your data", followed by exactly these nine `<h2>` headings, in this order and with this exact
   text: "Who we are"; "What we collect when you join"; "What goes on the public directory, and
   what never does"; "Why we're allowed to hold it"; "How long we keep it"; "Your data, your
   call"; "Who else sees it"; "Cookies and what we measure"; "If you're not happy".
2. Given the "Who we are" section, then it renders the controller identity string "Greenwich
   Parents & Carers CIC, company 16387545. Data controller: Aster Thackery." and the contact
   address `gpc.communitynews@gmail.com`.
3. Given the "How long we keep it" section, then it renders a visually distinct, clearly-labelled
   placeholder stating the retention period is pending controller sign-off — it contains the
   literal blocking token, never a fabricated number of days.
4. Given the "What goes on the public directory, and what never does" section, then its "Public"
   list contains exactly the FR-003 public field set (business name, category, description,
   website, Instagram, public enquiry contact) and its "Private" list states that the signup
   email, postcode and all survey answers are never published.
5. Given the "Your data, your call" section, then it renders a `RightsCallout` component
   containing the specified body copy and a real `<a href="/my-data">` link styled as a
   button, at least 48px tall, reading "Manage my data".
6. Given any of the five remaining sections ("What we collect when you join", "Why we're allowed
   to hold it", "Who else sees it", "Cookies and what we measure", "If you're not happy"), then
   each renders a clearly-labelled "content pending controller sign-off" placeholder rather than
   fabricated legal or compliance prose.
7. Given the page is rendered at a 320px viewport, then there is no horizontal scroll, heading
   levels are not skipped, and the page is reachable via the site's existing skip link.

## Dev Notes

<!-- LOCKED. Concrete guidance compiled from the planning docs WITH SOURCE CITATIONS.
     Cite every fact: [Source: prd.md#anchor] / [Source: architecture.md#anchor] /
     [Source: ux-design.md#anchor]. Label your own inferences [Inference]. -->

- **This is the launch gate.** "This requirement gates launch: the form does not go live before
  the page is updated." [Source: prd.md#FR-024] The page's structure can and must ship now; its
  legal prose is explicitly the controller's to write, not this story's
  [Source: epics.md#epic-8-data-rights-and-privacy — "Two blockers sit outside the backlog…
  FR-024's content is the controller's to author. Story 8.1 delivers the page; it cannot deliver
  the decision."].
- **The nine headings, in order, and what each must contain** are specified verbatim as a table:
  Who we are (controller identity + company number + `gpc.communitynews@gmail.com`); What we
  collect when you join (every category the form gathers — name, email, postcode,
  household/children, group and branch answers, the three free-text survey answers, business
  details, technical data for rate limiting, and the mail token with its rotation policy); What
  goes on the public directory (two lists, **string-identical** to the `/join` disclosure panel —
  "If the two ever diverge, the panel is the promise and the page is the breach"); Why we're
  allowed to hold it (lawful basis per purpose); How long we keep it (**BLOCKING** —
  `«RETENTION_PERIOD»` must become a real figure before launch, because the identical string is
  stored verbatim in every consent record); Your data, your call (the `RightsCallout` block); Who
  else sees it (processor list — Supabase, Brevo, the site host, the shared Google mailbox);
  Cookies and what we measure; If you're not happy (ICO route + contact)
  [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#7-data-rights-errors-and-edge-cases — Screen D-0].
- **Retention placeholder, not an invented figure.** ADR-010 states this exact blocking
  dependency: "the `hold_data` consent text still contains `«RETENTION_PERIOD»` (addendum Q8). It
  cannot go live as a placeholder — every day it runs produces records citing text GPC cannot
  evidence." [Source: architecture.md#adr-010-consent-records-are-append-only-and-version-bearing-enforced-by-trigger]
  Reusing the identical literal token on `/privacy` (rather than a different placeholder string)
  keeps both artefacts grep-able for the same open question. [Inference]
- **The `RightsCallout` copy is fully specified, not controller content** — it is UX-authored,
  reused verbatim, not something this story invents: "You can take your directory listing down,
  stop the newsletter, or ask us to delete everything we hold — whenever you like, without
  emailing anyone." + a `Manage my data` button (48px, real `<a href="/my-data">` so
  middle-click/open-in-new-tab work) + "We'll email you a link to make sure it's you. We aim to
  action every request within 20 working days."
  [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#7-data-rights-errors-and-edge-cases — Screen D-0 wireframe]. Container:
  `--radius-2xl`, 2px primary/30 border, `p-6` card, capped at `max-w-2xl` inside the prose column
  — no two-column treatment, "it would separate the promise from the button"
  [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#7-data-rights-errors-and-edge-cases]. Colour tokens generally per
  [Source: DESIGN.md#1-design-tokens].
- **The public/private list is a factual sync point, not legal prose.** FR-003 fixes the exact
  public field set: "business name, one category from the managed taxonomy, a short public
  description..., website, Instagram, and a public enquiry contact distinct from the signup
  email," and states explicitly "the signup email, postcode and all survey answers do not"
  become public [Source: prd.md#FR-003]. This mirrors the `/join` opt-in disclosure panel
  component [Source: DESIGN.md#2-component-specifications — §2.8 Public / Private Disclosure
  Panel], owned by story 3.5 — keep the wording in sync if 3.5 lands with different phrasing.
  [Inference]
- **This story does not touch `Layout.jsx`.** The public skip link already exists at
  `Layout.jsx:8-10`; its contrast fix (`#fc16a0` → `--color-primary-700`) is owned by story 6.1,
  not this one [Source: bmad-output/context/sharding-context.md#ux-rulings-that-override-the-long-form-drafts].
  This story only needs the link to already be reachable, which it is today.
- **Existing file to rewrite, not create.** `/privacy` already routes to
  `src/pages/PrivacyPolicy.jsx` [Source: inputs/codebase-constraints-brief.md#7-legalpolicy-constraints-already-published-on-the-site],
  registered in `src/App.jsx:56` — no route change needed.
- **Accessibility floor (NFR-008) applies:** every heading level present in order, no horizontal
  scroll at 320px, visible focus, ≥4.5:1 body contrast
  [Source: bmad-output/context/sharding-context.md#accessibility-floor-nfr-008--applies-to-every-ui-story].
- **Placeholder rendering is a structural requirement, not a suggestion.** The direction from the
  orchestrator for this story is explicit: ACs are about the page carrying the required sections;
  the content itself is the controller's to author. This story must not ship invented GDPR
  disclosure prose under any of the five open sections, even a "reasonable draft" — a shipped
  draft is easy to mistake for approved copy once merged. [Inference]

## Testing

<!-- LOCKED. STRATEGY ONLY — what to verify and how. NEVER run tests or quote coverage here. -->

- **Approach:** No automated test runner exists in this repo yet
  [Source: inputs/codebase-constraints-brief.md#1-stack--deployment-reality-non-negotiable — no
  TypeScript, no test tooling listed]. Verification here is a structural/manual review pass
  against the AC list, plus the manual accessibility pass NFR-008 already prescribes: "Automated
  audit plus a manual keyboard-only and screen-reader pass" [Source: prd.md#NFR-008].
- **Key scenarios:** heading order and text match AC1 exactly (copy-paste diff against the AC
  list); retention section never renders a number; public/private lists match the FR-003 field
  set field-for-field; RightsCallout link is a real anchor (not a button-styled `<div>` or
  JS-only navigation) and reaches `/my-data`.
- **Per-AC verification:** AC #1 → DOM inspection of heading tag order and text; AC #2 → visual
  check of the "Who we are" section against the quoted string; AC #3 → grep the rendered output
  for the literal `«RETENTION_PERIOD»` token and confirm no digit-based duration appears anywhere
  in that section; AC #4 → line-by-line diff of the two lists against FR-003's field set; AC #5 →
  keyboard-tab to the link, confirm it is an `<a>`, confirm computed height ≥48px; AC #6 → visual
  scan confirming every un-decided section shows the same placeholder treatment; AC #7 → resize to
  320px and confirm no horizontal scrollbar, then a heading-outline check (browser dev tools or an
  accessibility tree inspector) for skipped levels.
- **Fixtures / mocks:** none — this is a static content page with no data fetching.
- **Edge cases to cover:** the placeholder block must not be mistaken for real content by a quick
  visual scan (distinct styling, e.g. a dashed border or amber tint, not merely italic text);
  confirm `RightsCallout`'s link still renders correctly if `/my-data` does not yet exist in this
  environment (it should — a 404/blank result on click is a separate, already-known gap owned by
  story 6.1's catch-all route, not a defect in this story).
