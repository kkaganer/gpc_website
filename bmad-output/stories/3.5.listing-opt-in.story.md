# 3.5: Directory listing opt-in and the public/private disclosure panel

**Story ID:** 3.5
**Epic:** 3 — The public form
**Slug:** listing-opt-in
**Status:** ready-for-dev

## Story

As a **member deciding whether to be findable in the business directory**,
I want **a plain yes/no choice offered to anyone with a business regardless of which group I
picked, and — if I say yes — a disclosure that shows exactly which of my values will go public as
I type them**,
so that **I can decide with confidence, and never discover after submitting that something I
thought was private was actually published**.

## Acceptance Criteria

<!-- LOCKED. External dev tools must not edit Acceptance Criteria. -->

1. Given the member reaches the directory question, when it renders, then it is offered on the
   same terms to every member regardless of `groupChoice` (Business, Career or Both) and its
   copy states plainly that it is for anyone who runs a business or works for themselves,
   freelancers and sole traders included.
2. Given the member selects "Yes – list my business", when the section expands, then a public/
   private disclosure panel renders **first**, above the six listing fields, listing exactly the
   FR-003 public fields (business name, category, description, website, Instagram, public
   enquiry contact) under a "Public" heading and the private fields (first name, sign-up email,
   postcode, all survey answers) under a "Private" heading, and the panel's item text updates
   live as she types into the corresponding field.
3. Given the member selects "No, not right now", when she submits, then none of the six listing
   fields are requested and none are present in the submitted payload; any previously-typed
   listing values remain only in component memory and are not sent.
4. Given business name, website or Instagram were already answered in the Business branch (3.3),
   when the member opts in, then those three fields arrive pre-filled and independently editable
   in the listing section, with copy explaining they were copied from her business answers above.
5. Given the member types her sign-up email address into the public enquiry-contact field, when
   she blurs or after a 400ms debounce, then a warning appears and submission is blocked for that
   field until she either changes the value or ticks an explicit "I understand this address will
   be public. Use it anyway." confirmation; editing the value afterward clears both the warning
   and the confirmation tick.
6. Given the description field, when it exceeds 300 characters, then the counter flips to over-
   limit styling and text is **never truncated**; submission is blocked only at submit time, not
   while typing.
7. Given the category taxonomy is empty or still loading, when the member reaches the category
   field, then the field is disabled with explanatory helper text and the required rule on
   category is suspended rather than blocking submission.

## Dev Notes

<!-- LOCKED. Concrete guidance compiled from the planning docs WITH SOURCE CITATIONS. -->

- **FR-003 is this story's core contract:** opting in requests business name, one category from
  the managed taxonomy, a short public description (enforced character limit), website,
  Instagram, and a **public enquiry contact distinct from the signup email**; business name,
  category, description and enquiry contact are required, website/Instagram optional; declining
  means none of these fields are requested or stored; **eligibility is having a business, not the
  group answer** — offered to every member on the same terms; every listing has the same shape
  regardless of branch. [Source: prd.md#FR-003]
- **Screen D-1 copy, verbatim intro:** *"The directory is a public page on this site where local
  parents can find businesses run by GPC members. It's free, and you can ask us to take your
  listing down at any time."* Question legend: *"Would you like to be included in the GPC
  business directory? For any member who runs a business or works for themselves — freelancers
  and sole traders included. You don't need to have chosen 'Business Mums' above."* "At a glance"
  card: *"Six things go public. Your email, postcode and survey answers never do."*
  [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#2-the-directory-opt-in-and-consent — Screen D-1]
- **No focus move on "Yes".** The listing-details panel is inserted immediately after the
  opt-in fieldset **in DOM order** so the next Tab naturally reaches it; moving focus would rip a
  screen-reader user out of the radio group before she has heard her choice register. The reveal
  is announced once through the shared live region: *"Directory details opened. Six more
  questions below."* Selecting "No" instead announces *"Directory details closed. Your business
  details won't be submitted."* — and values stay in memory so re-selecting "Yes" restores them
  unchanged. [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#2-the-directory-opt-in-and-consent — Screen D-1 accessibility
  notes, decision-points table]
- **The "At a glance" summary card text is not admin-editable** — it is a factual statement about
  system behaviour; an admin editing it could make the disclosure untrue without anyone noticing.
  The intro paragraph and question label ARE admin-editable (FR-008). This story hard-codes the
  card's text rather than sourcing it from the config overlay.
  [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#2-the-directory-opt-in-and-consent — Screen D-1 accessibility notes,
  "Editability boundary"]
- **The disclosure panel — DESIGN.md §2.8, the signature component this story is named for.**
  Container: `--radius-2xl`, 2px `--color-info` border, `#dbeafe` fill, 16px padding. Heading
  "What other people will see" (H4, `--color-info`). Two lists, 🌍 Public / 🔒 Private, stacked
  mobile, side-by-side ≥768px, one field per line in the member's own words where possible. The
  private list is **never** collapsed, truncated, or behind a toggle — it is the reassurance, and
  hiding it defeats the component. **It updates live as she types**, echoing her actual entered
  values. Emoji are `aria-hidden`; headings carry the meaning. Colour is `--color-info` (blue),
  deliberately not brand pink — this is a factual guarantee, not a promotional element.
  [Source: DESIGN.md#2.8-public-private-disclosure-panel]
- **Heading ladder and markup, exact:** `<h3>` (section) → `<h4>` "What other people will see" →
  two `<h5>`s "Public"/"Private", each followed by a real `<ul>`. **Not a `<table>`** — the rows
  do not pair 1:1, and a table would invite a screen reader to read "Business name" and "Your
  first name" as related. The panel sits **above the first field and stays there** — FR-003 says
  "adjacent to the fields", and a disclosure that requires a tap has not been made. Verify in the
  manual NFR-008 pass that the "Public" `<h5>` is announced before its first item; if a tested
  reader skips it, add a visually-hidden `<li>` prefix rather than restructuring.
  [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#2-the-directory-opt-in-and-consent — Screen D-2, "The panel's content...
  accessibility notes"]
- **Screen D-2 field table, verbatim helper text, limits and types:**

  | Field | Req | Helper (above input) | Limit | Type |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Business name | ✔ | "Your business, practice or trading name — whatever you'd like shown at the top of your listing." | 80 | `text`, `autocomplete="organization"` |
  | Category | ✔ (suspended if taxonomy empty) | "Pick the one that fits best. Local parents use these to filter the directory." | must match a live category id | native `<select>`, generated from `directory_categories` |
  | Short description | ✔ | "One or two sentences about what you do and who you help. This is the main thing people read. Plain text only — links and formatting won't show." | **300**, rejected not truncated | `textarea`, no native `maxlength` |
  | Website | — | "You can leave off the https:// — we'll add it." | 200 | `url`, `inputmode="url"`, `autocomplete="url"` |
  | Instagram | — | "Your handle or the full link — either works." | 100 | `text`, `inputmode="url"` |
  | Email for enquiries | ✔ | "This is the address your listing will show, so anyone can see it. Use a business address, or a free address you're happy to make public — not the personal one you signed up with." / "Don't have one?   [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#2-the-directory-opt-in-and-consent — Screen D-2 field table]
- **The `Shown publicly` badge sits inside the `<label>`** for the enquiry-contact field, so the
  accessible name is "Email for enquiries, shown publicly, edit text, required" — public-ness is
  part of the accessible name, not a visual afterthought.
  [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#2-the-directory-opt-in-and-consent — Screen D-2]
- **Same-email match detection and its warning, verbatim:** trimmed, lower-cased comparison
  against the sign-up address, also matching after normalising `+tag` suffixes and — for
  `gmail.com`/`googlemail.com` only — after removing dots from the local part. Runs on blur and
  on a 400ms debounce. Warning body: *"If you use it here, it will be shown on your public
  listing where anyone — including people sending spam — can see it. Most members use a separate
  address for enquiries."* The warning is **advisory, never corrective** — it never rewrites or
  clears the field; editing to a non-matching value removes the warning **and resets the checkbox
  to unticked**, so an old confirmation can never silently authorise a new address. Record the
  acknowledgement as `enquiry_contact_matches_signup: true`/`false` for the moderator — **it is
  not a consent record**; do not write it into `member_consents`.
  [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#2-the-directory-opt-in-and-consent — Screen D-2, "Accessibility (non-
  obvious only)"]
- **Per-field error strings, verbatim:** Business name empty → *"Please add the name you'd like
  shown on your listing."* Category not chosen → *"Please choose a category so people can find
  you."* Description empty → *"Please add a short description — it's the main thing people
  read."* Description over limit → *"Your description is {N} characters too long. Please shorten
  it to 300 characters or fewer."* Website unparseable → *"That doesn't look like a web address.
  Try something like greenwichparentsandcarers.co.uk"* Instagram unparseable → *"That doesn't
  look like an Instagram handle or link. Try @yourbusiness"* Enquiry email empty → *"Please add an
  email address for enquiries — it's how people will reach you."* Enquiry email malformed →
  *"That doesn't look like an email address. Check for a typo."* Enquiry email = signup email,
  unconfirmed → *"Please either use a different email for enquiries, or tick the box to confirm
  you're happy for your sign-up address to be public."*
  [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#2-the-directory-opt-in-and-consent — Screen D-2 error table]
- **Website/Instagram normalisation is deliberately forgiving, not an error:** no scheme →
  normalised server-side (`greenwichparentsandcarers.co.uk` → `https://…`; `@handle` →
  `https://instagram.com/handle`); this story's client-side responsibility is to normalise on
  blur for display and not to block or error on a bare domain/handle — the server is the
  authoritative normaliser. [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#2-the-directory-opt-in-and-consent — decision
  points table]
- **D4 pre-fill (fixes source-form defect 1):** when B3–B5 (business name/website/Instagram from
  3.3) are already answered, this section arrives pre-filled and fully editable under: *"We've
  copied these from your business answers above. Edit them if you'd like them to read
  differently in the directory."* A Career Mum who opts in without ever answering B3–B5 types
  them fresh — this is the fix for the source form's defect where the directory question and the
  fields that would answer it were never connected. [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#1-joining--the-public-
  form — "D4"] [Source: prd.md#FR-003 — "a self-employed member who chose 'Career Mums' can
  list"]
- **Escape hatch on submit-time error:** if the member submits with the directory opted-in but
  listing fields empty, below the last field error an inline `Button` (`size="sm"`, from 3.1a)
  reads *"Not ready to write a listing? Change my answer to 'No, not right now'."* — activating it
  flips the radio to No, unmounts the listing section, clears its errors, and lets the form
  submit. This is the one escape hatch this story must wire; the surrounding error-summary
  mechanics belong to 3.7. [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#2-the-directory-opt-in-and-consent — decision
  points table]
- **Category taxonomy states:** in flight → `<select>` disabled, `aria-busy`, "Loading
  categories…"; zero rows (or a failed fetch) → disabled, "No categories available right now",
  helper text changes to *"We'll choose a category for you when we review your listing."*, the
  required rule is suspended and the member can still submit — the listing enters the moderation
  queue flagged `category_missing` for a moderator to set later (Epic 5's concern, not this
  story's, but this story must not block submission on it).
  [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#2-the-directory-opt-in-and-consent — decision points table]
- **Data minimisation (NFR-006):** exactly six fields and nothing else — every one in the FR-003
  public list. Nothing here is collected "for admin use"; a future non-published field belongs in
  the private member record, not this panel, because the panel's contract with the member is that
  *everything in it is public*. [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#2-the-directory-opt-in-and-consent —
  Screen D-2 accessibility notes] [Source: prd.md#NFR-006]
- **Category is a native `<select>`** so iOS uses the platform picker — a custom listbox would be
  a needless keyboard/VoiceOver risk on the one page that must not carry any.
  [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#2-the-directory-opt-in-and-consent — Screen D-2 accessibility notes]

## Testing

<!-- LOCKED. STRATEGY ONLY — what to verify per AC, test types, fixtures, edge cases. -->

- **Approach:** component tests for `PublicPrivateDisclosurePanel`'s live-echo behaviour and
  `DirectoryOptInSection`/`ListingDetailsSection`'s conditional mount/unmount, plus a manual
  keyboard and screen-reader pass over the heading ladder and the same-email warning flow.
- **Key scenarios:** typing into each of the six fields updates the corresponding panel line;
  declining after typing values keeps them in memory but excludes them from the payload;
  selecting "Yes" after 3.3's business fields were answered pre-fills business name/website/
  Instagram; typing the sign-up email into the enquiry field triggers the warning and blocks
  submission until either changed or the confirmation is ticked, and editing afterward resets the
  tick; the description counter reaches over-limit styling without truncating input; the category
  select degrades correctly through loading → populated and loading → empty.
- **Per-AC verification:** AC #1 → render with each of the 3 `groupChoice` values and assert the
  opt-in question and its copy are identical in every case; AC #2 → type into business name and
  assert the panel's "Public" list item for business name updates to the typed value within one
  render; AC #3 → opt in, type values, opt out, assert the serialized payload (whatever function
  3.8 will call) omits every listing key; AC #4 → answer B3–B5 in a stubbed branch state, opt in,
  assert the three fields' initial values match; AC #5 → type the stubbed sign-up email into
  enquiry contact, assert the warning and blocked state, tick confirm, assert it unblocks, edit
  the value, assert both warning and tick clear; AC #6 → type 310 characters, assert the input
  still contains all 310 (never truncated) and the counter reads "10 characters over the limit";
  AC #7 → mock an empty categories response and assert the select is disabled with the fallback
  helper and that a listing can still be marked valid without a category chosen.
- **Fixtures / mocks:** a stub `directory_categories` list (populated, empty, and
  loading/pending states) and a stub sign-up email from `useJoinForm`'s identity state (3.2).
- **Edge cases to cover:** a `gmail.com` sign-up address matched against a dot-variant or `+tag`
  variant typed into the enquiry field; a Career Mum with no B3–B5 values opting in (fields start
  genuinely empty, not pre-filled with `undefined`/`null` strings); rapid opt-in/opt-out toggling
  before the pre-fill effect settles.
