# 3.3: Group choice, branch reveal and discard-on-change

**Story ID:** 3.3
**Epic:** 3 — The public form
**Slug:** branch-rendering
**Status:** ready-for-dev

## Story

As a **member choosing which group to join**,
I want **the form to show only the Business questions, only the Career questions, or both,
based solely on my answer, and to warn me before discarding anything I've already typed if I
change my mind**,
so that **I never answer a question that doesn't apply to me, and I never lose an answer without
being told**.

## Acceptance Criteria

<!-- LOCKED. External dev tools must not edit Acceptance Criteria. -->

1. Given "Business Mums" is selected, when the form re-renders, then the 7 Business questions
   (B1–B7) appear and no Career question is present anywhere in the DOM, the tab order, or the
   heading tree.
2. Given "Career Mums" is selected, then the 4 Career questions (C1–C4) appear and no Business
   question is present anywhere in the DOM, the tab order, or the heading tree.
3. Given "Both" is selected, then all 11 branch questions appear as two sibling sections
   (Business then Career).
4. Given a branch was answered and the member changes the group answer such that at least one
   already-typed field in the abandoned branch would be discarded, when the change is made, then
   a confirmation dialog names the count of answered questions in that branch and blocks the
   change until confirmed; cancelling reverts the radio selection and discards nothing.
5. Given the member confirms a branch change, when it completes, then the abandoned branch's
   fields are **deleted from the answers state object** (not blanked to empty strings), so they
   are absent — not present-and-empty — from anything later serialized into the submission
   payload.
6. Given a transition from Business→Both or Career→Both (a superset), when the change is made,
   then no confirmation dialog appears and no answer is discarded.
7. Hidden-branch questions are never validated as required and can never block submission while
   their branch is not mounted.

## Dev Notes

<!-- LOCKED. Concrete guidance compiled from the planning docs WITH SOURCE CITATIONS. -->

- **FR-002 is this story's contract, verbatim ACs:** Business selected → 7 Business questions,
  no Career question; Career selected → 4 Career, no Business; Both → all 11; changing the group
  after answering a branch discards that branch's answers so they are **absent** from the
  submitted payload; hidden-branch questions are never required and never block submission.
  [Source: prd.md#FR-002]
- **Branch content is unmounted, never `hidden`.** A `hidden` fieldset is still a payload risk
  and a maintenance trap — the guarantee is that there is nothing in the DOM, tab order, heading
  tree, or payload to hide, disable, or filter at submit time.
  [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#1-joining--the-public-form — "The zero-business-questions proof"]
- **The abandoned set, precisely:** `branchFields(old) \ branchFields(new)`. Business→Both and
  Career→Both abandon nothing (superset) — **no dialog**. The four other transitions
  (Business→Career, Career→Business, Both→Business, Both→Career) show the dialog **only if at
  least one abandoned field has a value** (a non-empty trimmed string, a selected radio, or ≥1
  checked box). **Directory fields are never in the abandoned set** — once pre-filled per D4 they
  belong to a section the member can see independently, and silently emptying visible fields
  would be the exact surprise this dialog exists to prevent.
  [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#1-joining--the-public-form — Screen S2, "FR-002 discard rule"]
- **Dialog copy, verbatim shape:**
  > **Change your group to Career Mums?**
  > You've answered 3 of the 7 business questions. Changing your group will clear those answers.
  > Nothing is saved and nothing is sent to GPC.
  > *[only when the directory section holds values]* Your directory listing details will stay as
  > they are.
  > `[ Keep Business Mums ]`  `[ Clear and change ]`

  The count is **real and computed**, not a fixed string — singular reads *"You've answered 1 of
  the 7 business questions."* [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#1-joining--the-public-form — Screen S2]
- **On confirm:** abandoned fields are deleted from the state object (never set to `''`); their
  errors drop; the summary (3.7's job, but this story must not leave stale error state behind)
  recomputes; sections renumber and `«N»`/`«T»` recompute (3.2's `LengthSignal` consumes this);
  focus returns to the radio the member acted on — **never into the new section**, which would
  hide the consequence of their own choice; the shared live region announces once: *"Business
  questions removed. Career questions added, 4 questions. This form now has «N» questions, about
  «M» minutes."* **On cancel:** the radio reverts and the live region says nothing — a cancelled
  action is silent. **No undo is offered** — the dialog *is* the undo, which is why Cancel holds
  initial focus. [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#1-joining--the-public-form — Screen S2]
- **Keyboard trap to be aware of, deliberately not "fixed":** the radio group is one tab stop and
  arrows move-and-select natively, so arrowing from Business to Career **can open the discard
  dialog on a single key press.** That is intended — the dialog is the guard, and cancel restores
  the previous value, so no keyboard user can destroy answers without a second explicit action.
  Do not switch to arrow-moves-without-selecting. [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#1-joining--the-public-
  form — Screen S2 accessibility notes]
- **Dialog contract (DESIGN.md §2.9, adjudication A14) — build to this spec exactly:**
  `role="dialog"` + `aria-modal="true"` + `aria-labelledby` pointing at the title; focus moves to
  the dialog on open (to the first interactive element, or the heading if the first control is
  destructive); focus trapped (Tab cycles within); Escape closes and returns focus to the
  triggering control; background content is `inert`/`aria-hidden` while open; the backdrop is not
  the only dismissal route. Mobile: bottom sheet, full width, `--radius-2xl` top corners only.
  Tablet+: centred, `max-w-lg`, `--shadow-xl`. Extended props beyond the existing `ConfirmModal`:
  `confirmLabel`, `confirmTone` (`primary | destructive`), `requireReason`.
  [Source: DESIGN.md#2.9-modal-dialog]
- **Cross-epic scope note — read before implementing:** `epics.md` assigns "one Dialog primitive"
  to Epic 5, story 5.1 (`shared-admin-chrome`), and `ConfirmModal` is expected to be rebuilt on
  top of it there. Epic 3 has no dependency on Epic 5 in the epics.md dependency graph, and this
  story needs a working confirmation dialog now. **This story therefore creates
  `src/components/ui/Dialog.jsx` as the canonical shared primitive.** Story 5.1 must **reuse this
  file** (and rebuild `ConfirmModal` on top of it there) rather than recreate a second `Dialog`
  component. Flagged to the orchestrator as a cross-epic sequencing gap in epics.md, not resolved
  by this story alone. [Source: epics.md#epic-5-moderation-and-shared-admin]
  [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#dev-handoff-notes — "Cross-cutting work that belongs to no single
  section"] [Inference — the resolution above is this story's proposed fix, not a cited decision]
- **Field set, from the source-form inventory (registry-owned, this story renders it generically
  rather than field-by-field):** Business (7) — B1 stage (radio), B2 industry (text), B3–B5
  business name / website / Instagram (all optional; URL scheme normalised on blur, not
  rejected), B6 "What are you currently looking for?" (18-option `ChoiceChipGroup`), B7
  challenges (optional long text, no character counter — a counter on an optional question
  implies a target). Career (4) — C1 current situation (10-option chips, de-duplicated from the
  source form's 11, which repeated "Looking for work" — the fix lives in the seeded config, not
  in this component), C2 industry, C3 support that would help, C4 challenge (optional). Both:
  Business section then Career section as sibling cards; nothing is merged — "Which industry are
  you in?" and "What industry do you work in?" are different questions and stay separate so
  FR-014's insights split survives. [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#1-joining--the-public-form — Screen S3
  "Fields"]
- **B3–B5 are also FR-003's fields — do not duplicate them here.** D4 (pre-fill, not re-ask):
  when a member later opts into the directory (3.5), the directory section copies these three
  values and lets her edit them independently; this story only needs to write the business
  section's own values to `useJoinForm`'s state in a shape 3.5 can read from — it does not build
  the pre-fill UI itself. [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#1-joining--the-public-form — "D4"]
- **Render generically off the code registry, per ADR-008** — `src/lib/join/questions.js` (owned
  by Epic 2 story 2.1; read-only here) is the structural source of which questions belong to
  `business`/`career`, their `type`, and `maxLength`; label/help/options come from the published
  config (2.2). The renderer should be a switch over known `type`s, not one hand-built component
  per question key — this is what lets a future admin-added question (within the registry) render
  without a new component. [Source: architecture.md#adr-008-form-configuration-code-defined-
  registry-database-overlay-and-versioned-publish]
- **Sections are `<section>`, not `role="region"`.** Deliberately no `aria-labelledby` landmark
  per section — it would add rotor stops for information the heading tree already carries. Focus
  is **never** moved into a newly mounted branch on selection; the live-region announcement is
  what orients a non-sighted member. [Source: EXPERIENCE.md#1-joining--the-public-form — Screen
  S3 accessibility notes]

## Testing

<!-- LOCKED. STRATEGY ONLY — what to verify per AC, test types, fixtures, edge cases. -->

- **Approach:** hook-level unit tests for the abandoned-set computation and discard logic in
  `useJoinForm`, component tests for `GroupChoiceSection` + `Dialog` interaction, and a manual
  keyboard pass covering the arrow-key-triggers-dialog case.
- **Key scenarios:** each of the 3×2 = 6 group transitions (Business/Career/Both → each other
  state) against the dialog-or-not rule; discard deletes keys rather than blanking them; a
  superset transition (→Both) never shows a dialog even with answered fields; a transition with
  zero answered fields in the abandoned branch skips the dialog even though it's a narrowing
  transition.
- **Per-AC verification:** AC #1/#2/#3 → select each group value and assert the DOM's business/
  career question count matches 7/0, 0/4, 7/4 respectively, and that no career `label` text is
  present when Business is selected (and vice versa); AC #4 → answer 1 business field, switch to
  Career, assert the dialog renders with "1 of the 7 business questions" and that confirming is
  required before the Career section mounts; AC #5 → confirm a discard and assert
  `Object.keys(answers)` no longer contains the abandoned keys (not merely `answers[key] === ''`);
  AC #6 → answer fields then switch Business→Both, assert no dialog and all business answers
  survive; AC #7 → attempt to submit with Career mounted only and assert no business-branch
  validation error appears anywhere (this AC is fully provable only once 3.7 exists — this story
  proves it structurally by confirming the unmounted branch contributes no `required` fields to
  whatever validation function 3.7 will call).
- **Fixtures / mocks:** a stub registry/config shaped like Epic 2's contract for the 11 branch
  questions, so this story is not blocked on 2.1/2.2 shipping first.
- **Edge cases to cover:** rapid double-toggling between two groups before a dialog's confirm
  click resolves; a member who answers a business field, clears it back to empty, then switches
  group (no dialog, since the abandoned set is empty by value not by "was ever touched");
  Escape-closing the dialog behaves identically to clicking Cancel.

## Learnings from Previous Stories

- 3.1a/3.1b's `RadioGroup` already implements native arrow-moves-and-selects semantics — this
  story relies on that, not a custom one. (from 3.1a, 3.1b)

  keep it isolated to `Dialog.jsx` rather than leaking dialog semantics into field-level errors.

  subsequent story extends in sequence — this story is the second to touch both; expect the same
  from 3.4 onward. (from 3.2)
